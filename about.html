ABOUT THE WORKSPACE

Clear news. Five stories at a time.

Punjabi News Workspace is a daily news-reading workspace for people who want a concise view of important developments in Pakistan and around the world. Each edition is designed around five selected stories, with English and Urdu reading options.

What we cover

Daily editions may include government and public affairs, the economy, education, technology, science, cricket, sports, culture and other developments relevant to readers in Pakistan.

Sources and attribution

Published story cards include the source name and a link to the original publisher when that information is available. The source link lets readers consult the publisher’s full report and context. Punjabi News Workspace is a reading and curation platform, not a replacement for the original publishers.

Languages

The workspace currently provides English and Urdu versions of its daily stories. Readers can switch languages from the main website.

Editorial approach

Stories are presented as concise summaries with source attribution. Readers should use the linked publisher report for full details, updates and additional context. If a source corrects or updates its report, the publisher’s version remains the primary reference.
